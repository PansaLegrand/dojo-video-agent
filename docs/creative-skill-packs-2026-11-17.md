## Scope

Three self-contained packs teach branded endings, chart storytelling and festival
motion. They contain method and art-direction guidance, not fixed compositions.
The existing read_skill index loads bodies only on request. Pack frontmatter
supports name and description; names must match folders. No executable loading,
community installer, recursive dependency loader or new provider jobs are added.

Folder packs and flat Markdown share one bounded loader. New local runs retain an
exact private skill snapshot so file changes cannot alter their resumed guidance.
Older builds keep their original kit archive and do not acquire the new pack names.

## Acceptance cases and gates

- Short exact-URL endings append after the complete existing timeline; portrait,
  long identity and calmer follow-up cases preserve original content and edit IDs.
- Animated bar/line charts preserve supplied labels, values, units and proportional
  encoding; missing data produces a question rather than invented claims.
- Birthday and formal company-event instructions produce appropriate distinct
  treatments, evaluated from actual rendered output without prescribed layouts.
- Loader bounds, unsupported metadata, symlinks, required-tool eligibility, saved
  guidance and paid-response recovery are tested as executable behavior.
- Run npm run check, current-profile evaluation, actual browser/decoded exports,
  commercial consumer checks and worker protocol/build checks where changed.

The failed commercial anniversary and monthly-chart trials ended with honest

a centered flex parent plus absolute child offsets placed the milestone text
below the 720-pixel canvas. The chart's first attempt duplicated node IDs; a

child instead of a timeline item. Trying to change that requirement after it
was frozen was correctly rejected. Separate diagnostic renders do not count as
accepted model edits.

The repeated anniversary succeeded on its other attempt; this does not erase
the failure. The final runner exits nonzero for retained failed trials even
though the complete matrix finished.

The earlier three campaigns used `gpt-6.1-sol`. Each stopped on one request with
unknown usage after the unchanged 180-second deadline. Their retained traces
identified append-helper misuse, an unsupported easing name, ending fade-out,
pixel coordinates in normalized shape points and root-ID completion mismatches.
Focused skill/prompt guidance corrected those authoring mistakes; validators and
the deadline were not weakened. The final campaign used the separately recorded
`gpt-6-sol` model binding. Model and guidance changes are confounded, so this is
not a controlled model-performance comparison.

## Separate rendered review

Two independent saved-output review revisions sampled **all 30 exported records**,
including all six charts marked needs-review and both before/after calmer pairs in
each host. They inspected decoded frames, exact final-frame holds, and chart
geometry. This is sampled visual review, not continuous human playback of every
frame. Original automated grades were not rewritten.

- All four percentage line charts map 25/50/75% correctly onto their stated
  0–100 scale. The first commercial monthly chart has proportional bar heights
  of 108/180/144/252 pixels (9 pixels per unit).
- The first OSS monthly line chart has proportional settled points (8 pixels
  per unit), but its scaled entrance briefly misses its fixed data markers.
  Its numeric Y domain is also undisclosed. Correct final values do not establish
  a coherent chart animation.
- The first OSS long-URL portrait ending is exact and unclipped but too small:
  its glyphs occupy only 286 × 17 pixels on a 720 × 1280 canvas. This is a visual
  quality failure despite an automated pass.
- Birthday and anniversary treatments adapt visibly to their occasions.
  One commercial anniversary adds an unrequested “excellence” promotional claim;
  the creative brief does not substantiate that wording.
- The inspected calmer edits preserve identity and timing while visibly slowing
  the reveal, at the cost of a shorter fully readable hold.

Revision 2 reviewed the final ten records without finding an additional text or
clipping defect. Both second-repeat portrait URLs were larger than the weak first
OSS result. Exact final-frame samples cover all 30 exports. The reviewed work is
useful simple motion graphics, with the concrete shortcomings above; neither
technical export success nor automatic checks establish broad creative acceptance.

`test-results/creative-skills/live04-independent-review-content-01/review.md`
and `review.json` for the first 20 IDs, source hashes and measurements; the

## Acceptance boundary

The feature is implemented and exercised in both local application paths.
Short instructions produce editable results and can drive materially different
birthday versus company-event treatments, with the visual defects above still
requiring improvement. These outputs remain simpler
than the hand-directed README showcases. They do not establish consistently
showcase-level art direction, complete automatic chart grading, or production
acceptance.

R27 remains an **evaluated development candidate with retained failures**, not an
accepted production rollout. Next work is reliable native layout and completion
requirement authoring, broader visual quality review, and a separately scoped
release decision. Validation remains intact.
A skill is guidance selected dynamically by the agent; it is not a fixed output
template, a tool installer, a general community marketplace, or new authorization
to spend on external generation.
