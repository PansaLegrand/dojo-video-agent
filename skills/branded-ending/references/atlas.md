# A reveal that earns its final frame

Rows show three original 12-second studies; columns show successive moments.
Borrow a motion relationship, not sample branding, artwork or a complete layout.

1. **Assembly:** separate meaningful pieces, establish how they fit, then resolve
   them into a coherent lockup. For a URL outro, colored planes or strokes can
   gather around a reserved text area and become a final brand mark/underline.
   Never separate or alter the exact domain into misspelled letter fragments.
2. **Depth:** an architectural passage creates a direction of travel, then opens
   into quiet space for the identity. A few nested planes and parallax can convey
   depth in 2D; an actual orbit or sculpture can use Three. Make the camera/frame
   change serve the reveal, and stop movement enough for the URL to be read.
3. **Typography:** scale, spacing and contrasting type treatments create distinct
   beats. Preserve a stable final domain text node. Masking bands, a moving frame,
   or a shape that hands focus to the type can be more memorable than a glow fade.

## Compose a short closing sequence

For a four-second appended ending, aim for a purposeful transition into a visual
gesture, its resolution into the identity, and a generous final reading hold.
Examples: a ribbon sweeps across the frame and folds into a corner lockup; three
planes rotate/slide into alignment and reveal the URL; a broad typographic field
contracts into a precise final wordmark. Adapt the method to the project instead
of reproducing this list in order.

In a premium or calm brief, one precise transformation is enough. In “cool,” bold
or playful briefs, explore a stronger silhouette/scale change before settling.
Avoid treating a rounded border + underline + centered domain, or glowing text
alone, as the automatic complete answer. Both can be ingredients in a richer idea.

A long URL is the hero, not a footnote. Judge it at phone display size; use the
available width and sensible line breaks at natural URL boundaries if necessary,
without changing characters. Remove decoration before shrinking the identity to
tiny type. Keep it fully visible through the final frame.
