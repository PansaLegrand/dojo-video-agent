# Spatial composition before detail

The board shows successive moments from three original MIT engine studies.
Reason about scale, focus and motion; these objects and words are not mandatory
templates or project assets.

1. **Miniature world:** buildings, ground, roads and small moving elements form a
   scale system. The camera reveals the whole before detail draws the eye. Adapt
   that hierarchy with distinct forms; object quantity is not a visual concept.
2. **Assembly:** layers separate far enough to explain their relationship and
   return to a resolved object. Preserve part identities. For invented celebratory
   geometry, this can become a cake assembling, a gift opening or an abstract
   emblem resolving around native text.
3. **Camera and depth:** nested structures establish a direction of travel.
   Foreground and background motion differ, creating parallax and a focal region.
   Let the camera lead somewhere, then leave a stable readable ending.

Light should reveal form. Use a clear key/fill relationship and material contrast
that survives the actual background. Metallic dark objects can disappear without
suitable light; more geometry will not fix that. Check the widest pose for clipping
and the final identity for contrast.

Keep important text native and exact. Recompose twelve-second examples for the
user's interval without rushing the hold. Read installed grammar for supported
camera/motion units. Keep global project format unchanged unless requested.

Sources: `city`, `precision`, `cinema`; exact frames and video hashes are in
`provenance.json`.
