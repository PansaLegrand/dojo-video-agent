# Editorial motion with honest geometry

Rows 1–2 are moments from the original Field Notes data study; row 3 is a software
workflow illustration. All sample figures and names are illustrative. Never copy
them into a user's chart. Borrow hierarchy, timing and grouping, not their data.

1. **Orient, encode, emphasize:** establish a title and scale, reveal marks from
   a common baseline, then move attention to a supported comparison. Keep exact
   values readable after the motion settles. One meaningful highlight is stronger
   than making every bar a different neon color.
2. **Explain relationships:** introduce entities, connect them, then emphasize a
   path. This works for flow/process diagrams when the user supplied that meaning;
   decorative connectivity must not invent causation between numerical categories.
3. **Group and reorganize:** objects can enter as a collection, sort into groups,
   and resolve into a structured view. Preserve identities and values throughout.
   For dense data, use successive panels with a stable scale and an explicit scope.

## Design the explanatory action

Ask what motion makes easier to understand. Bars can grow together from zero,
then a bracket or subtle focus region can explain the largest supported difference.
A line can draw through its actual data points in order, with markers and labels
arriving at the drawing front. A process can move one token through named stages.
The structure should still be clear if the decorative background is hidden.

Do not scale a complete line about its center while its markers stay fixed: the
intermediate geometry disconnects from the data. Use coherent segment reveals,
clip/wipe mechanisms supported by the installed grammar, or synchronized points.
Keep the numeric domain visible when a line's baseline is not zero. Never use
elastic magnitude overshoot, 3D perspective or a camera tilt to exaggerate data.

Give the chart an editorial setting: strong type hierarchy, an intentional light
or dark surface, one accent and generous plot margins. Bold art direction belongs
around truthful marks, not in distorted numeric encoding. A changing title alone
does not make the chart animated. Final values need a substantial reading hold.
